# FUNIX — AI Operating System Master Index

**Release and current facts:** Consult `FUNIX_REGISTROS_VIVOS.md` for the applicable release, dated operating facts, and authorized decision records. If the release is not recorded as approved, do not present this text as an effective company policy.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This repository is FUNIX's **conceptual reference system** for aligned decisions across strategy, identity, economics, customers, sales, operations, compliance, digital infrastructure, and brand expression. Its intended readers are FUNIX leadership, authorized collaborators, and AI systems supporting decisions; it is not an employee repair manual.

It is not a substitute for current operational records, signed agreements, technical work instructions, live financial reports, source code, or qualified legal review. The existence of a documented ambition or designed workflow does not prove that it has been implemented.

The system has eleven domain files (`00`–`10`). Read the relevant authoritative file and cross-check dependencies rather than treating any isolated statement as an instruction to override other requirements.

## 2. Stable purpose and strategic direction

**Mission:** Transform a moment of insecurity into an experience of trust.

**Economic aim:** Build a sustainably profitable and resilient automotive body repair and paint business.

**Strategic direction:** Develop operating efficiency as an intended competitive advantage through processes, capable people, management discipline, appropriate technology, and continuous learning. When validated, improvements should enable consistent repair quality, compelling customer value, productive capacity, and reinvestment.

**Important qualification:** Higher productivity does not itself create demand or profit. A gain must be captured through more accepted profitable work, avoidable cost reduction, improved mix, less rework, reliable capacity, or another demonstrated mechanism. Pricing, margin, and reinvestment decisions are made deliberately, not mechanically.

The full business thesis, strategic priorities, and stage-dependent sequencing have **one conceptual owner: `10_strategy.md`**. This index states only the common orientation.

**External experience:** FUNIX seeks to be technically competent, accessible, clear, honest, predictable, and competitive on value, without luxury theater or discount-led promises. The original integrity principle remains: *"The customer will never notice" is never a reason to compromise repair quality.*

## 3. Important distinctions

| Do not confuse | Required interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Profit with efficiency | Sustainable economic value is the objective; efficiency is a preferred means, not the only legitimate source of value. |
| Productivity with deliveries counted | Compare like-for-like repair work and resources. Until a reliable equivalent-work unit is calibrated, report by repair family and describe mix limitations. |
| Capacity with demand | Determine whether the active constraint is acquisition/conversion, production, cash, parts, management capacity, or a combination. |
| Volume with scale economics | More cars, people, bays, or simultaneous jobs do not establish lower unit cost. |
| Quality with limitless refinement | Technical integrity and the accepted service specification are mandatory. Extra refinements are separately scoped; unclear standards must never be used to excuse defects. |
| Price sensitivity with poor fit | Customers can compare prices rigorously without accepting poor quality. |
| Competitive pricing with price leadership | Competitive value is a goal to verify by comparable quotes and actual unit economics, not a universal public claim. |
| Standardization with inflexibility | Standardize repeatable steps and control points while applying appropriate technical judgment to variable repairs. |
| Software specification with deployed capability | Designed, tested, live, and reliably adopted are different states. |
| Strategic ambition with authorization | A possible 5,000 m² operation or industrial layout is not an approved investment or fixed target. |

## 4. Information status and evidence

Classify material statements using the following mutually distinguishable labels:

1. **Enduring principle** — intended standing value or behavior.
2. **Approved policy/decision** — explicitly authorized, with scope, approver, and effective date.
3. **Current observation** — dated report or observation, not necessarily instrument-measured.
4. **Historical estimate** — earlier approximate figure, not assumed current.
5. **Working hypothesis** — plausible mechanism or design requiring evaluation.
6. **Developing capability** — planned, prototyped, partially tested, or rolling out.
7. **Measured result** — defined metric, time period, calculation, source, and responsible person.
8. **Future ambition** — desired destination without operational or capital authorization.

If the category, date, owner, or supporting source is unknown, explicitly mark the relevant aspect **unverified**. Do not turn reported facts into measured outcomes or an ambition into an approval.

### Current reality register

Maintain a **small, dated register of operational facts** separate from enduring conceptual files. Each record should contain:

`Fact / value or status | Unit or definition | As-of date | Evidence/source | Responsible person | Confidence or validation status | Next review`

Start with information actually needed to decide: staffing and critical skill coverage; facility space; weekly delivered jobs by family; active work and delays; received/approved quotes; price comparisons; revenue, variable costs and cash; deployment state of Coda, n8n, WhatsApp, quoting and AI workflows.

**No current baseline is asserted by this index.** Original tickets, channel shares, and historical throughput are context until dated evidence verifies their present relevance. Space, staffing and throughput are kept in the live register; the possible future larger facility remains a conditional ambition in `10_strategy.md`.

The provisional location is **`FUNIX_REGISTROS_VIVOS.md`, Section B**. A responsible person must be designated and records validated before an entry is treated as authoritative. This register may later be migrated to an approved system without duplicating facts here.

## 5. Binding limits, precedence, and conflicts

Apply **one precedence procedure**, in this order:

1. **External mandatory requirements:** applicable law, occupational and customer safety, mandatory technical requirements, and consumer rights always govern. Documents cannot waive these. Seek qualified advice when interpretation is consequential or uncertain.
2. **Binding internal conduct, authorized commitments and procedures:** non-negotiable integrity/behavior boundaries in the *approved* `01_identity.md`, valid customer approvals and contracts, approved policies and delegated operational procedures, and applicable technical/service-quality acceptance criteria. No economic calculation or unapproved domain suggestion overrides them.
3. **Decision method:** use `09_decision_framework.md` to evaluate alternatives **within the binding limits above**; it never creates an exception to them.
4. **Strategic and functional interpretation:** use the approved `01_identity.md` for identity, `10_strategy.md` for thesis and priorities, and the file designated as owner of the specific concept in Section 6. These are complementary scopes, not competing hierarchies.
5. **Current evidence and non-approved guidance:** use the dated live register for facts and non-approved domain guidance as proposals only. Newly validated observations may supersede historical estimates **as factual evidence**, not silently repeal policy.

**When multiple rules apply at the same level:** enforce the more protective mandatory/customer/quality limit until the actual competent authority resolves the conflict; never treat an unapproved suggestion as a binding rule.
**When standards or authority are missing:** do not invent a threshold, relax quality, grant an unapproved discount, or approve an irreversible expense. Continue the current authorized safe practice where feasible; hold or escalate customer-affecting exceptions to authorized FUNIX leadership and, for technical acceptance, the **named technical authority recorded in the live register**. If technical conformity is materially uncertain and that authority is unavailable, do not mark the vehicle as approved for delivery; promptly inform the customer and obtain appropriate technical/legal guidance for any requested handover. Safety concerns require immediate protection and technical review. Record the unresolved decision.

A conflict is resolved by identifying the exact statements, statuses and scope, applying the sequence above, presenting feasible options if needed, and recording an explicit decision. Revisions must be propagated to all affected references.

- **Expansion:** test demand, unit economics, cash, management capacity, process reliability and risk before committing capital (`02`, `05`, `09`, `10`).

## 10. Release and change control

The **effective release** of this repository must be identified in **`FUNIX_REGISTROS_VIVOS.md`, Section A**, by a dated release record, with approver, list of exact file versions, scope, and any superseded release. A filename or conversational acknowledgement alone is not evidence of policy adoption.

Each change should record: reason, evidence/decision, owner, affected concepts and files, and approval status. Preserve historical versions and avoid reproducing dynamic status banners in permanent content.

AI systems must not claim a particular draft is operative without a corresponding explicit release decision.

## 11. Core instruction for users and AI systems

- Distinguish current observation, hypothesis, measured evidence, and approved policy.
- Respect law, safety, technical integrity and explicit customer commitments before optimization.
- Use `09` for economic trade-offs; efficiency is a strategic means, not an automatic override of other value sources.
- Do not assume price leadership, a fixed ideal production layout, a deployed software capability, or verified present throughput.
- Prefer narrow, testable, reversible changes where uncertainty is meaningful.
- Seek sustainable economic results with consistent quality and a trustworthy customer experience.

**Final principle:** FUNIX intends to be technically responsible, economically strong, increasingly efficient, and reliably accessible—not merely bigger, more complex, or more impressive-looking.
