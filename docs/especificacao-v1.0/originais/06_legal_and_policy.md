# FUNIX — Legal & Policy Guardrails

**Release and applicability:** Consult `FUNIX_REGISTROS_VIVOS.md` for the approved release and legally reviewed procedures. This conceptual document is **not a legal opinion, a signed contract, an approved guarantee or proof of regulatory compliance**. Legal assertions and forms require validation against applicable current law and the facts of the service.

## 1. Purpose and boundaries

This file owns **legal, ethical and compliance safeguards** for quotations, approvals, vehicle custody, technical integrity, guarantees, data use, labor/safety, suppliers and public claims. It does not reproduce the business strategy (`10`), pricing model (`02`), commercial steps (`04`), repair acceptance standards (`05`), system specifications (`07`) or brand execution (`08`). Apply the common precedence rule in `00_master_index.md`; do not establish a second competing hierarchy here.

**Operational principle:** Efficiency means doing required work correctly with less avoidable waste. It never authorizes weakened customer rights, undocumented scope changes, insecure data practices, unsafe shortcuts, concealed defects or labor violations. Embed proportionate compliance controls in normal work.

## 2. Accurate claims and defensible promises

FUNIX must distinguish **measured result, reported observation, intention, preliminary evaluation and approved commitment**. Do not make unsubstantiated claims such as “always cheapest,” “best in Brazil,” “perfect repairs guaranteed,” “fastest in the city,” or “final diagnosis from photographs.” The ambition of competitive pricing is not proof of leadership in comparative price or quality.

Customer-facing information should be accurate about:

- actual repair scope, materials, significant exclusions and limitations;
- what may change after inspection or disassembly;
- price, payment conditions and applicable approved quote terms;
- credible dates, dependencies and externally controlled approvals;
- warranty terms actually approved and legally applicable.

Marketing, quotations and automated messages cannot promise capabilities that the current organization has not verified. `08` owns brand expression, `04` owns commercial communication and `05` owns technical capability.

## 3. Estimates, quotations and approval of work

Distinguish **preliminary guidance**, **draft internal pricing**, **customer-facing quotation**, **approved scope** and **approved amendment**. Photos and customer descriptions can support initial guidance, but material uncertainty about hidden damage, safety, scope, price or time may require physical inspection or further investigation.

For Brazilian consumer services, the Consumer Defense Code (CDC), including **Article 40**, contains requirements for prior budgets and approved changes. Any customer-facing form should be checked by qualified counsel for applicable required details, including labor, materials/equipment, payment conditions and service start/end dates. Do not treat a standardized template as automatically compliant.

Maintain a traceable connection between the approved version of a quotation and the authorized work order. As applicable, identify the vehicle/customer, itemized services and materials, price and conditions, timing assumptions, validity and evidence of customer approval. Never intentionally under-scope to win the sale or charge undisclosed additions.

If a new finding materially affects authorized work:

1. Document the issue and distinguish urgent protective measures from additional paid services.
2. Explain technical options and consequences for cost and timing.
3. Obtain appropriate internal and **customer authorization before the paid change**.
4. Preserve the original version and the revised accepted scope.
5. Update production instructions and customer communication.

Customer authorization, marketing image permission and the legal basis for data processing are different things. A single generic checkbox must not be presumed to satisfy all purposes. Electronic approvals, including WhatsApp, require an appropriate evidence and legal assessment for their specific use.

## 4. Vehicle receipt, custody and return

Use a proportionate, attributable record of vehicle condition and handover: date/time, delivered/received by, identifying details, visible damage and relevant irregularities, keys and articles in custody, meaningful odometer/fuel information, special instructions and any exceptional condition. Document movement to third parties or test drives under the appropriate authorization process.

The reception and delivery checklists are **evidence and operational control**, not blanket waivers of consumer rights or excuses to omit inspection. Preserve the link to the work order, photographic records when appropriate, material exceptions, technical release and actual handover. Retain only information justified by the service and legal needs.

## 5. Technical integrity, parts and accepted quality

Safety, suitable methods, approved technical standards, honest parts descriptions, durable workmanship and authorized scope are mandatory. Never treat defects as acceptable because they are hidden or because the customer is price-sensitive.

`05_operations.md` owns quality acceptance by repair family and technical inspection. While criteria or responsible authorities remain incomplete, apply **the interim quality rule in `09_decision_framework.md` Section 3**: do not reclassify a suspected defect as discretionary refinement; obtain technical review; if material conformity remains uncertain and the designated authority is unavailable, do not mark the repair approved for delivery and communicate the issue. Legal/customer obligations apply regardless of the label of a quality tier.

Parts categories, substitutions and subcontracted work must satisfy applicable law and suitability requirements, including the consumer-protection rules that may apply under **CDC Article 21**. Obtain legal/technical validation of actual policy and required consumer disclosures before adopting a generalized rule.

## 6. Guarantees, complaints and rework

Separate **mandatory legal protection** from **additional contractual warranty**. Under Brazilian consumer rules, a voluntary contractual guarantee does not replace statutory rights; specific written terms require legal validation. The original intention of an approximately **one-year** contractual guarantee remains **an unapproved historical proposal**, not a current binding FUNIX promise in this document.

Before announcing contractual warranty terms, approve and document covered services/parts, applicable timeframes and conditions, claim routes, valid exclusions, subcontractor responsibilities and a written customer-facing instrument. Do not promise “lifetime” or “unlimited” warranty without an appropriate validated structure.

For complaints: register the account and urgency, protect against immediate safety risks, preserve evidence, review technically, assess rights and commitments with qualified support where needed, communicate a response, and feed confirmed problems into root-cause learning. Neither a signed checklist nor a customer's collection of the vehicle automatically removes legal protections. Nor is every complaint necessarily a proven FUNIX defect.

## 7. Customer data, privacy and digital systems

FUNIX may handle names, contact information, vehicle identifiers, photos, messages, voice content, payments, complaints, employee access and other personal information. The **LGPD** applies according to context, legal bases and processing purposes; **contractual approval is not synonymous with consent under the LGPD**. Any simplified treatment for small processing agents requires checking its actual applicability.

Necessary design controls include:

- purpose limitation and proportionate data capture;
- clear notices and appropriate lawful bases;
- role-based access, credential management, recovery and third-party access review;
- defined retention/disposal and appropriate response to data-subject requests;
- protection of photos, identifiers, transcripts and system logs;
- incident procedures and vendor/AI data-sharing assessment;
- traceable high-impact customer and staff decisions without keeping everything indefinitely.

`07_digital.md` owns technical implementation, integration safeguards and actual deployment verification. AI may organize, summarize or propose; it must not invent customer consent, authorize paid work, publish private content, make unsupported legal/technical declarations, erase required evidence or silently replace required human decisions.

**Unresolved for validation:** formal privacy information, processing inventory, retention policy, role access, vendor arrangements and incident-response procedure. Design work alone is not proof of implementation.

## 8. Truthful marketing and customer evidence

Use real facilities, real services and authentic testimonials with valid permissions and nonmisleading presentation. Service-record photographs are not automatically cleared for advertising. Do not fabricate reviews, manipulate before/after images to suggest results not achieved, reuse another shop's work as FUNIX's, or publish identifying material improperly.

Comparative price claims require **current, materially equivalent and documentable** comparisons, including scope, parts, timing, payment conditions and warranty coverage. Avoid fake urgency, concealed conditions and deceptive discount anchors. Claims and image permission review belong here; creative and site expression belong to `08`.

## 9. People, suppliers, physical facilities and environmental responsibilities

Production targets must respect employment and occupational-safety requirements, competent supervision, safe tools, training, appropriate workloads and fair treatment. Do not reward output in a way that encourages hidden defects, suppressed incidents, falsified logs or unsafe practices. Staffing, skill coverage and operational supervision belong to `05`; employment instruments and material workplace-risk questions require qualified review.

Suppliers and third-party operators should have clear scope, applicable standards, custody handling, approvals, responsibilities and traceable exceptions. Insurance, association and institutional workflows may contain third-party constraints: never promise approvals, reimbursements or deadlines FUNIX does not control.

Body repair/painting may trigger specific requirements involving chemicals, solvent handling, ventilation, painting booths, waste, emissions, noise, fire/electrical protection, environmental licenses and facility conditions. Competent specialists must validate applicable requirements before materially modifying methods, equipment or facilities. Do not invent local permits, legal dates or technical thresholds.

## 10. Disputes and escalation

Handle claims, collections and conflicts with factual reconstruction, respectful communication and defensible documentation. Do not threaten, harass, fabricate liability conclusions or use custody of a vehicle or personal information as coercion without legal review of the exact circumstances.

**Escalate the affected action** to competent commercial, technical, legal, labor, privacy or environmental support when uncertainty has material safety, customer-rights, payment, employment, contract, privacy, supplier or regulatory consequences. Protect people immediately if there is an active hazard; do not block unrelated safe work unnecessarily. Scope and decision authority follow `09` and the live authority register, not invented local thresholds.

## 11. Pending approvals and implementation

Prioritize legal/compliance validation by potential harm and current exposure, not by exhaustive paperwork. High-impact unresolved subjects include: lawful quote/acceptance forms, actual technical-release and custody controls, guarantee wording, approved parts/substitutions, staffing safety, privacy notices and vendor controls, and current facility obligations. Record **individual actions, responsible persons and review dates only in `FUNIX_REGISTROS_VIVOS.md`, Section C**, prioritized by `10_strategy.md`; do not maintain a competing backlog here.

A policy draft may express intent, but only properly authorized, published documents establish an adopted internal procedure. This file does not assert that any pending guarantee, legal form, privacy process, certification or workplace compliance has already been validated.
